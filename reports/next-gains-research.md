# team research before full training

- **research question:** after integrating our entity-resolution methods, which experiments could improve source1 set decisions before full training?
- **scope and date:** supplied challenge rules, our team's saved measurements and internal implementations, and primary research; 2026-09-26. no external business-identity data.

## conclusion

**measure where the trained retriever's recovered links disappear, then test calibration and ownership on cached full-pool scores.** our bounded replay measured old-gate losses on learned candidates; it was not an end-to-end test of the new gate. this dated research prioritized experiments 1–3 before full training. the development score of **0.98454661** is distinct from the team-reported public **0.987** and leading **0.9907** at that stage. ## evidence that changes the priority

- the organizer scores **per-source 1 macro f0.5**, including a full point for a correctly empty set. it requires the **actual final pre-matcher candidate set**; *this pipeline* exports its actual postgate, pre-cross-encoder candidates. pair recall, in-candidate f0.5, and format validation cannot establish the final score. [primary local audit](../artifacts/retrieval-validation/report.json#L9-L174) (not tracked); [retriever's fitting contract](../src/retr.py#L168-L191). the [filtering benchmark](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.12521v5#S3) distinguishes nearest-neighbor recall from er pair completeness and identifies filtering as a recall ceiling.
- the present stack uses raw-text/corpus evidence but cached scores omit retrieval-derived columns; `src/stack2.py:42-63` drops placeholder columns. the new gate can use dense scores and reciprocal rank; the *planned* reverse lane uses blank-address-only **approximate** ivf search. do not infer its recall from top-50 forward search. ## bounded cpu gate-loss pilot: measured, not a new model

the unchanged 54-feature lexical gate was run via `train.load_models` and `tfeat.prep/make` on the **same saved learned candidate unions** as raw e5-small cosine, with all candidates of each target kept together. tie order is source1 id. the original retrieval searched complete country reference banks; the replay loads only candidate reference texts. each country has 2,000 fold-0 anchors and **full anchor truth degrees**: india 6,925 links, 256 linked blank-address targets, 120 true empty anchors; us 6,902 links, 308 blank, 99 empty. neither scorer was fit or thresholded; fold-1 labels were not used. ## shared evaluation contract

keep fitting owners in fold 2; use fold-0 partition 0 for a small stack fit, partition 1 for calibration/choice, and partition 2 for a **paired development comparison**. all aliases follow their reference owner. report exact per-reference f0.5 over **every** chosen reference, including true empty sets and *all* its truth links in the denominator. score every competing source 1 candidate of each incident target **before** selecting one owner; build name counts, score context, or sibling evidence on the complete relevant raw/candidate population **before** projecting references for evaluation. compare the same frozen candidate sets unless testing retrieval. fold 0 was used in earlier development and fold 1 was already inspected: neither is a new blind audit. ## defer or avoid

1. **more near-identical cross-encoder runs by seed alone:** our inspection of 13 kaggle `CONFIG` dicts verified that most use `max_length=128`, `batch_per_gpu=40`, `lr=2e-5`, and `max_pairs=3_000_000`; these are code-verified settings rather than accuracy evidence. the trainer freezes word embeddings, and our earlier second model added about **0.00021 in-candidate holdout f0.5**. we therefore prioritized error diversity over additional seeds. [frozen embeddings](../src/ce.py#L89-L104) · [different evaluation units](../Ml_Challenge.txt#L112-L119).
2. **projected sibling/graph evidence or owner-filtered validation:** removing incident targets before building the confident-owner bank changes competitors' siblings; the local miniature reproduction changes the feature. build context on the full population or do not use it. [reproduction](additional-workspace-findings.md#L18-L32).
3. **hard france rules or unqualified histogram correction:** france has no training truth; pooled positive-density transfer and small histogram cells can overcorrect. an earlier raw-france variant lost **0.0018 public** against its calibrated variant; this remains a pipeline-specific observation. [calibration assumption](../src/post.py#L170-L204).

## assumptions and practical limits

the candidate oracle is an upper bound **for its evaluated candidate set**, not for test or a changed gate. expected-set decoding assumes independent calibrated pair probabilities, although aliases and owner conflicts are dependent. density transfer assumes stable positive score mass; a shifted generator can violate it. all results above are saved measurements, code properties, or explicit proposed tests, not new runs or a leaderboard forecast. with the deadline near, promote a change only on the same full-target, entity-grouped comparison; verify that final matches remain within the actual scored candidate file. no external identity lookup enters the pipeline; any later model must meet the organizer's mit/apache-2.0 and size constraints.
